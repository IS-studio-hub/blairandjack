About us

We Share Your
Lived Experience

Blair & Jack is the brainchild of Dr. Bimpe Ayeni, a board-certified plastic surgeon, and her husband Ade Ajayi. It was created after Ade struggled to find a skincare solution that soothed painful irritation and bumps from shaving. After consulting with a pharmacist and formulating together, the brand — and its hero product, the Anti-Bump Treatment — was born.
